Key concepts explained in plain language

What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small files stored by websites on your device to remember preferences, login sessions, and browsing behavior.
What Is Browser Fingerprinting?
Browser fingerprinting is a tracking method that combines details about your device, browser, and settings to identify you.
What Is a Data Broker?
A data broker collects, packages, and sells personal information gathered from public records, apps, purchases, and online activity.
What Is End-to-End Encryption?
End-to-end encryption means only the sender and receiver can read the message, not the service provider in between.
What Are Tracking Pixels?
Tracking pixels are tiny invisible images or snippets of code used to tell when a page or email has been viewed.
What Is a VPN?
A Virtual Private Network creates a secure tunnel for your internet traffic, hiding your real IP address and location from websites and your internet provider.
