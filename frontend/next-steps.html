Protect Your Privacy

Simple, practical steps you can take today to reduce your data exposure

01

Use a Privacy-Focused Browser

Switch to Firefox or Brave, which block trackers by default. They stop most invisible tracking without any extra effort.

Easy High Impact
02

Install a Tracker Blocker

Add uBlock Origin to your browser. It blocks ads and trackers, making pages load faster while protecting your privacy.

Easy High Impact
03

Review App Permissions

Check which apps have access to your location, contacts, and microphone. Remove permissions that aren't necessary.

Easy Medium Impact
04

Use a VPN on Public Wi‑Fi

A VPN encrypts your connection, preventing others on the same network from seeing your browsing activity.

Medium High Impact
05

Clear Cookies Regularly

Set your browser to clear cookies when you close it, or use a cookie auto-delete extension.

Easy Medium Impact
06

Limit Social Media Tracking

Adjust privacy settings on social platforms. Disable off-platform activity tracking in Facebook, Instagram, and similar apps.

Medium High Impact
07

Use Separate Browsers

Use one browser for social media and another for general browsing. This prevents cross-site tracking between the two.

Medium Medium Impact
08

Check Your Digital Footprint

Search your name online and request removal of unwanted personal data from data broker sites.

Hard High Impact
